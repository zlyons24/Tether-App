# Delete Your Tether Account

**Last Updated: March 7, 2026**

If you would like to delete your Tether account and associated cloud data, email **zach.lyons24@gmail.com** from the email address associated with your account.

## What to Include

Please include:

- The email address used for your Tether account
- A short note that you want your account deleted

## What Will Be Deleted

When your deletion request is processed, we will delete the cloud account data associated with your Tether account, including synced profile information, synced journal data, synced AI conversation history, synced saved scans, and other synced app data tied to your account.

## What May Be Retained

We may retain limited records if required for legal, security, fraud prevention, tax, or billing purposes.

## Timeline

We aim to process deletion requests within 7 business days.

## In-App Data Controls

You can also delete certain data directly inside the app from Settings, including clearing journal data or clearing all app data on your device.
